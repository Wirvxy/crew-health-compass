Changes made for this app:
- The four textures used (base colour, normal, roughness, metallic) were
  downscaled from 4096x4096 to 1024x1024 PNG to reduce download size.
- The displacement texture is not included.
- The FBX model file (source/astronaut.fbx) is used unmodified.

Provenance note: the Sketchfab page describes the model as "Cool astronaut I
found!", so the original creator has not been verified. The credit above
follows the license shown on the Sketchfab page.
