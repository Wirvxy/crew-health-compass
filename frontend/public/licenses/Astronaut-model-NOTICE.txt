The author describes it as their own work, made in Blender with textures
painted in Adobe Substance Painter (published 30 May 2024).

Our copy came from a re-upload of the same model by Javi_DaviYT
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/astronaut-287589e324b942b8be57dbd6c539b2ab).
It matches the original's published details: about 19.6k triangles, a
Blender 4.1 export, and 4K textures with Substance Painter's default names.

Changes made for this app:
- The four textures used (base colour, normal, roughness, metallic) were
  downscaled from 4096x4096 to 1024x1024 PNG to reduce download size.
- The displacement texture is not included.
- The FBX model file (astronaut.fbx) is used unmodified.
